← all demos 06 · Steps Media, then question, then answers. Each part keeps its entrance.

Look for

  • Steps => media | fade | question | slide-up | answers. A transition name sits between two parts and applies to what follows.
  • DefaultSteps and DefaultStepTransition in the header do the same for every question without its own list.
  • What is not named joins the last step, so nothing is lost.
  • A video that ends counts as "next step". Clicking Next step or pressing the next-question key works too.
  • Revealing the answer shows everything at once.
