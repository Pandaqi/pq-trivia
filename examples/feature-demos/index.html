PQ-Trivia feature demos

One small page per feature. Every quiz is plain text inside its own page, so you can read the source, change a line and reload.

Serve the repository with bun run preview and open http://localhost:4173/examples/feature-demos/. Most demos also work from file://; the language, theme and widget ones need a server, because a browser refuses to fetch local files.

Content

  • 01 · Basics Start, rounds, end, the eight everyday types, inline text.
  • 02 · Media Images, audio, video, captions, a transcript, one watch limit.
  • 03 · Advanced types Hotspot, diagram, buckets, matrix, parts, estimate, rank.
  • 04 · Flow When, Unlock, Continue, hidden blocks and presenter notes.
  • 05 · Planning Pools, difficulty, variants, shuffling, filters, a secret theme.
  • 06 · Scoring Partial, per-correct, negative, streaks, wagers, lifelines, teams.

Look and motion

  • 07 · Layout Named layouts, built-in classes, backgrounds.
  • 08 · Animations Every screen transition, and the answer reveal.
  • 09 · Steps One part at a time, with its own entrance.
  • 10 · Timers Per question, per round, whole quiz, beeps and expiry.
  • 11 · Theming A theme folder, tokens, colour schemes, backgrounds.

Host and platform

  • 12 · Languages Two files, one quiz, switch while playing.
  • 13 · Extend A sandboxed script widget, custom actions, the event stream.
  • 14 · Export JSON, CSV, GIFT, QTI, handout, share link, class analytics.
  • 15 · Live Host, player and watcher, teams, reveal modes, QR.
