← all demos 05 · Animations Every screen transition, one per question. Then the reveal.

Look for

  • Transition animates the screen as it arrives. Press next and watch the new slide come in.
  • Reveal animates the correct answers when the answer shows.
  • Names: fade, slide-up, slide-left, slide-right, zoom-in, zoom-out, flip-x, wipe, blur-in, shake, pulse, none.
  • What the names look like is the theme's job. Paper ships the keyframes; a theme can redraw them.
  • Turn on your system's reduced-motion setting and all of it stops. That is a feature, not a missing demo.
